# GOOGL: Long Thesis and Scorecard (as of 2026-10-14)

## 1. Thesis

**Position:** Long Alphabet (GOOGL)

**Thesis statement:** Alphabet is becoming a full-stack AI compounder. Google Cloud (including TPUs) is now a second profit engine growing about 80% YoY with a USD 514B backlog. Search keeps growing in the mid-teens through the shift to AI. Together these support about 20% revenue growth with operating margins of 33% or more, which should outrun the dilution from a capex cycle above USD 180B a year. At about 35x trailing core earnings the stock prices in some of this but not all of it. The thesis is wrong if Cloud growth or margins roll over, or if Search starts shrinking, while capex keeps rising.

### Pillars
1. **Search holds up through AI.** AI Overviews/AI Mode and Gemini keep Search & other revenue growing at least 10% YoY. No query or monetisation erosion shows up.
2. **Google Cloud becomes the second engine.** Cloud grows more than 40% YoY, operating margin is above 25%, and the backlog keeps building, including TPU system sales and large AI-lab contracts.
3. **Full-stack AI advantage.** Gemini models plus in-house TPUs give lower cost per token and real product traction (Gemini app MAU, API token volume, enterprise uptake).
4. **Operating leverage survives the capex cycle.** Group GAAP operating margin stays at 32% or higher despite rising depreciation.
5. **Capital returns and per-share value creation.** FCF funds capex plus buybacks, so the diluted share count keeps shrinking and FCF per share grows.

### Risks
1. **Capex outruns returns.** 2026 capex guidance is about USD 195-205B (one source says USD 180-190B), and 2027 is guided higher. TTM FCF is already down 20.2% YoY, and Q2-26 FCF was negative.
2. **Search disruption.** Chat-based assistants (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Apple) take commercial queries, or AI answers compress ad load and pricing.
3. **Regulation and antitrust.** Remedies in the US search case (default deals, data sharing) and the ad-tech case, plus EU DMA enforcement.
4. **Balance-sheet and dilution shift.** The 1H-26 common equity issuance (USD 30.4B), USD 19.0B of 6.25% mandatory convertible preferred, and higher long-term debt (USD 98.2B vs 23.6B a year earlier) reverse the net-buyback story.
5. **Earnings quality and concentration.** TTM net income is inflated by about USD 136B of 1H-26 gains on equity securities, so headline P/E understates the multiple. Cloud backlog may also be concentrated in a few AI-lab customers.
