# GOOGL – Long Thesis and Scorecard (as of 2026-10-14)

**Position:** Long Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) | **Price:** $353.78 (close 2026-10-13) | **Target:** $430.00 (+21.5%) | **Stop-loss:** $290.00 (-18.0%) | **Conviction:** Medium

## Thesis statement
Long GOOGL: Google Cloud has become a second profit engine (Q2 2026 revenue +81.8% YoY at a 35.6% segment margin) on top of a Search business that is still growing double digits through the AI transition. That combination should support mid-20s% core EPS growth into 2027 and justify about 32x core earnings, even with a capex cycle near $200,000M that has temporarily wiped out buybacks and free cash flow.

## Pillars (falsifiable)
1. **Cloud hypergrowth with margin expansion.** Cloud growth stays above 40% YoY and the segment operating margin stays above 25% through 2027.
2. **Search stays resilient to AI disruption.** "Google Search & other" keeps growing at least 10% YoY, showing that AI Overviews and AI Mode add to monetization rather than cannibalize it.
3. **Group margins hold despite rising depreciation.** Consolidated GAAP operating margin stays at or above 32% (FY2025 level) as depreciation from the capex ramp comes through.
4. **Capex earns its return and capital returns resume.** TTM FCF stays positive, buybacks restart by 2027, and the diluted share count turns flat or falling.

## Risks (what would invalidate it)
1. **Capex overbuild / AI-ROI disappointment.** 2026 capex guidance is $195,000M–$205,000M (secondary source), and Q2 2026 quarterly FCF was -$5,855M. If Cloud demand slows, depreciation crushes margins.
2. **Search share loss to chat assistants** (OpenAI, Meta, Apple, Perplexity), or lower query monetization.
3. **Regulation and antitrust.** Possible remedies from the US DOJ search and ad-tech cases, EU DMA enforcement, and the end of default-placement payments. Status not verified this session.
4. **Earnings-quality and valuation optics.** Reported trailing P/E (17.8x) is flattered by a $99,031M Q2 unrealized equity gain. On a core run-rate the stock trades near 33x. If equity marks reverse, reported EPS falls sharply.
5. **Dilution and financing.** New debt issuance ($56,226M gross in 1H26), a new mandatory convertible preferred, and zero buybacks pushed diluted shares up 0.9% YoY.

## Conviction: **Medium**
Pillars 1–3 are on track or ahead. Cloud is the strongest data point in the thesis, and it supports staying long. Conviction is held at Medium, not High, because Pillar 4 is clearly deteriorating: quarterly FCF turned negative, buybacks are zero, the share count is rising, and the headline P/E is distorted by equity gains. The upgrade trigger is Q3 2026 Cloud growth above 60% together with 2027 capex commentary that implies positive FCF. The downgrade trigger is any break of the stop-loss conditions.
