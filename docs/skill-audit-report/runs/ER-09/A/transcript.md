## Methodology
- **TTM revenue growth** = TTM revenue vs the prior-year TTM, as reported on the trailing-financials page.
- **FCF** = operating cash flow minus capex (stockanalysis definition). Stock-based compensation is **not** deducted.
- **FCF margin** = TTM FCF / TTM revenue.
- **EV/TTM FCF** uses the published EV. If FCF is negative the ratio is NM and ranked last.
- **Rule of 40** = TTM growth % + FCF margin %.
- **Composite** = the average of the four ranks (1 = best). Lower EV/FCF ranks better. The four metrics have equal weight. Rule of 40 double-counts growth and margin on purpose, which tilts the score toward quality.

## Long ideas

**1. NVDA (Long): the highest-quality compounder, at a market multiple of FCF**
- It is #1 on growth (83.4%) and Rule of 40 (125.3), with a 41.9% FCF margin. At 43.2x EV/FCF it trades at about the same multiple as AVGO, below META, MSFT and GOOGL, and roughly 1.2x AAPL's multiple while growing about 6x faster.
- The capex its customers are spending (the reason hyperscaler FCF is falling) shows up as NVDA's revenue.
- **Key risk:** hyperscaler capex digestion or a cut. Revenue is concentrated in a handful of buyers whose own FCF is now thin or negative (AMZN, ORCL), so an air pocket would hit growth and the multiple together.

**2. ADBE (Long): a cash machine priced as if it were in terminal decline**
- 40.8% FCF margin, 12.0% growth and a Rule of 40 of 52.8, at **8.8x EV/FCF**, the cheapest multiple in the universe by far. That is about an 11% FCF yield on EV, with net debt close to zero.
- The market is pricing generative AI disruption of the creative-seat model. TTM growth is still double-digit, which argues against that outcome so far, and the buyback shrinks the share count fast at this yield.
- **Key risk:** a value trap. AI-native tools (from model labs, Canva and others) compress seat counts and pricing power, and growth falls to low single digits. SBC is a meaningful offset to reported FCF.

**3. CRM (Long): margin-led Rule of 40 at 15x FCF**
- 34.5% FCF margin and 11.2% growth give a Rule of 40 of 45.7, at 15.0x EV/FCF. It is #2 on valuation and #4 on margin.
- Operating discipline has turned it into a cash-return story. An agentic-AI monetization option (Agentforce) is priced at close to zero.
- **Key risk:** seat-based CRM erodes as AI agents replace users, and acquisitive M&A brings back dilution or debt. EV is about USD 31,000M above market cap.

*Runner-up:* AVGO (#2 composite, Rule of 40 92.9, 44.2x) was left out only to keep the long book diversified away from a second AI-semi name. On the numbers it ranks ahead of ADBE and CRM.

## Short idea

**INTC (Short): a USD 540,000M market cap on USD 2,831M of FCF**

- The price reflects a foundry turnaround and strategic-investor support, not current economics. Capex needs keep FCF thin for years.
- **Key risk:** a squeeze driven by policy or partners (government ownership, strategic investments, a marquee 18A/14A foundry customer win). Fundamentals may not matter for a long time. Size small and use defined-risk structures.

*Watch (not shorted):* ORCL. Its TTM FCF is -USD 28,720M and Rule of 40 is -18.4, with EV about USD 132,000M above market cap. The capex is backed by contracted backlog, so the short case depends on financing and counterparty risk rather than the screen alone.
