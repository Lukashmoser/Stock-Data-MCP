## Top 3 catalysts most likely to move the group
1. **Hyperscaler earnings, 2026-10-28/29 (MSFT, META, GOOGL, then AMZN).** Their 2027 AI capex guidance sets the demand outlook for NVDA and AMD, and these four names make up most of the group's market cap. MSFT and META are trading near their 52-week highs, so the bar is high.
2. **FOMC decision, 2026-10-28 14:00 ET.** The 10-year yield is about 5.2%, so the rate path is what drives multiples. The decision comes out about two hours before three mega-cap reports.
3. **September CPI, 2026-10-14 08:30 ET.** This is the first inflation print in the window. It sets FOMC pricing and the direction of yields going into earnings.

Runner-up: AMD Q3 on 2026-11-03, the last AI-semis print before NVDA, which is estimated for 2026-11-18.

Separately, the search-case remedies from 2025 rejected a Chrome divestiture.
