# Amazon.com (AMZN): Q3 2026 Earnings Preview

**Quarter:** Q3 2026 (quarter ending 2026-09-30)
**Expected report date:** 2026-10-29 (Thursday), after the close, call at 17:00 ET. This is an estimate: Amazon had not confirmed the date in any source found. It follows the usual pattern; Q3 2025 was reported on 2025-10-30.
**Price:** $258.58 at the close on 2026-10-13 (MarketBeat). The stock is up about 12.4% YTD as of 2026-10-02 (Trefis).

## 1. Setup in one paragraph
Amazon goes into the print after a very strong Q2. AWS grew 36.7%, its fifth straight quarter of faster growth and the fastest rate in 18 quarters. Operating income of $27.5B beat the top of guidance, and the stock rose 15.3% the next day. The bar is now high. Q3 guidance is soft on the surface: revenue of $197.0-202.0B (+9-12%) and operating income of $22.5-26.5B. Prime Day moved into Q2, which management says costs about 400 bps of Q3 growth, and FX is an 80 bp headwind. On the other side, 2026 capex was raised to about $220B, and TTM free cash flow is now negative (-$7.6B). The print will be judged on AWS growth against 36.7%, the Q4 operating income guide, and whether capex goes up again.

## 5. What will decide the reaction
1. **AWS growth against 36.7%.** Holding at 35% or more, alongside capacity additions, is what the stock needs. A drop to the low 30s would hurt it, even if management blames capacity limits.
2. **Q4 operating income guide.** The holiday quarter carries cost headwinds: the U.S. minimum wage for operations staff rose to $20/hr in September 2026, and fuel and driver costs are up. A Q4 range centred below consensus would undo a Q3 beat, as happened with the 2026-02-05 print.
3. **Capex and free cash flow.** Capex is about $220B for 2026 and TTM FCF is -$7.6B. Any first steer on 2027 capex, and how the spend is funded, could swing the stock more than the P&L does.
4. **Retail margins after the Prime Day shift.** North America and International margins in a quarter without Prime Day will show whether the retail productivity gains (operating margin of about 13.7% in Q2) hold. Advertising growth is the offset.
5. **Quality of earnings.** Headline GAAP EPS may again be swollen by Anthropic gains, or hit by mark-downs. The Street will focus on operating income and EPS excluding gains, so a headline "beat" on EPS alone will not move the stock.
