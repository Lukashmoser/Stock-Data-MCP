## 5. What will decide the reaction

1. **AWS growth vs. 36.7%.** Five straight quarters of acceleration have driven the stock. Holding at 35% or above, plus a backlog update, is the base case. Growth below about 32% would break the narrative.
2. **Capex and FCF.** Q2 capex was 54,208 and TTM FCF was negative. The market sold off in February on capex. Updated 2026 and early 2027 capex commentary, and whether AWS margin (about 39% in Q2) holds against depreciation, matter more than the EPS number.
3. **Q4 (holiday) guidance.** Revenue and operating income ranges vs. the Street, and how much incremental AI and Kuiper spend they absorb.
4. **Retail margin after the Prime Day shift.** Whether North America margin stays near 8% without Prime Day, and how tariffs and pricing are developing.
5. **Quality of earnings.** Anthropic marks will distort GAAP EPS. Watch operating income and AWS operating income instead, and expect noise in headline EPS comparisons.
