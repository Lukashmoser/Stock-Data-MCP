**Growth.** Meta is the fastest-growing platform at scale:

- Reddit grew faster still (66.6%), but from a base about 1/80th of Meta's.

**Positioning.** The five platforms follow three business models:
- **Intent:** Alphabet.
- **Discovery and social graph:** Meta, TikTok and Reddit.
- **Closed-loop commerce:** Amazon and TikTok Shop.

Meta covers the whole funnel and is strongest in performance and app-install ads for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). Its moat is the scale of its first-party engagement data, combined with AI ranking and compute.

**AI.**
- **Meta looks like the biggest net winner.** In Q2 2026, ad impressions rose 14% and average price per ad rose 12%. Advantage+ has passed a USD 75B annual run-rate. Meta reports ranking gains of +8.3% in Facebook ad clicks and +15.7% in conversions.
- **Alphabet is on the defensive but holding up.** AI Mode is a risk to clicks, yet Search & other still grew 16.8% in Q2 2026.
- **Amazon and TikTok Shop have the best measurement.** Their attribution is closed-loop, while Meta relies on modeled conversions.

**Financials.**
- **Meta:** gross margin 81.7%, R&D 31.4% of revenue, S&M 5.5%, SG&A 12.3%. Market cap is about USD 1,914,859M, based on the 2026-09-25 close.
- **Reddit:** gross margin 91.4%, R&D 30.0%, S&M 23.0%, SG&A 33.2%. Market cap is USD 30,770M at the 2026-10-12 close.
- **ByteDance:** the latest priced deal valued it at USD 550,000M (Reuters, 2026-02-25). Secondary trades reportedly reached about USD 600,000M in April 2026.

**Synthesis.**
- The main debate about Meta is cost, not demand:
  - 2026 capex guidance is USD 130-145B and expense guidance is USD 165-169B.
  - Q2 2026 operating income fell year on year, from USD 20,441M to 18,775M. A USD 2.4B legal charge and USD 1.2B of severance contributed.
- The biggest competitive threats are:
  - **ByteDance/TikTok** for attention. The US joint venture closed on 2026-01-22, which removes the ban overhang.
  - **Amazon** for measurement.
  - **Google AI Mode** for capturing intent.
- **What to watch:** Q3 2026 revenue against Meta's USD 61-64B guidance, and the mix between price per ad and impressions.
