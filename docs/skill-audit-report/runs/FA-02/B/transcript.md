## Final reply

**Meta in digital advertising: competitive landscape** (full report: `Meta_Digital_Advertising_Competitive_Analysis.html` in the run folder)

**Bottom line.** Meta is the fastest-growing scaled ad platform. TTM revenue to 2026-06-30 was USD 228,247m, up 27.7%. That is about twice the growth of Google advertising (14.0%) and ahead of Amazon ads (24.2%). AI ranking and Advantage+ automation are lifting both volume and price: in Q2 2026 impressions rose 14.0% and price per ad 12.0%. The risk is on the cost side, not demand. Meta guides to USD 130,000–145,000m of 2026 capex, and Q2 2026 operating income fell 8.0% even though revenue rose 28.0%.

**Positioning.**
- **Alphabet** owns intent through Search, plus YouTube and CTV. Google advertising was USD 315,346m TTM, up 14.0%.
- **Amazon** owns closed-loop purchase data, plus Prime Video and sports. Advertising services were USD 76,050m TTM, up 24.2%.
- **ByteDance/TikTok** owns the youth interest-graph feed and TikTok Shop. Revenue was about USD 186,000m in FY2025, up about 20.0% (estimate); about 60% of it is ads.
- **Reddit** owns intent-rich communities. TTM revenue was USD 2,779m, up 66.6%, which is about 1% of Meta's scale.
- **Meta** owns identity at very large scale (3.60bn daily active people) and the lower-funnel SMB performance budgets.

**Financial comparison (TTM to 2026-06-30).**
- **Meta:** gross margin 81.7%, R&D 31.4% of revenue, S&M 5.5%, operating margin 38.1%, market cap USD 1,942,473m (8.5x sales).
- **Reddit:** gross margin 91.4%, R&D 30.0%, S&M 23.0%, operating margin 28.2%, market cap USD 29,877m (10.8x sales).
- **ByteDance:** cost lines are not disclosed. The latest private valuation is about USD 480,000m (2025-11 secondary sale, about 2.6x sales).

**How AI is changing targeting and measurement.**
- Ranking now runs on large models: Meta's Generative Ads Recommender, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode ads, Amazon's Ads Agent and sponsored prompts, TikTok's Smart+.
- Meta cites Facebook clicks +8.3%, conversions +15.7%, and Advantage+ above a USD 75,000m run-rate.
- Automation bundles targeting, bidding and creative into one product. Advertisers then rely on incrementality tests, MMM and clean rooms to check platform reporting.
- First-party, closed-loop data owners gain. Amazon holds the purchase data; Meta holds identity plus server-side conversions.
- AI search is a threat to Reddit's Google referral traffic and changes the click economics of Google's own search ads.

**Strategic synthesis.**
- **Alphabet:** high threat, stable.
- **Amazon:** high threat, rising. It is the fastest-growing large ad business and has the data edge.
- **ByteDance/TikTok:** high threat, uncertain. It competes most directly for attention, and US restructuring adds risk.
- **Reddit:** low threat.
- **Meta's levers:** Threads and WhatsApp monetization, plus AI agents for businesses.
- **Meta's exposures:** capex return, no owned commerce data, regulation, and Reality Labs losses (about USD 19,190m in 2025).
- **What to watch:**
  - Q3 2026 results (guidance USD 61,000–64,000m) and whether price per ad holds up.
  - The 2026 expense guide of USD 165,000–169,000m.
  - The TikTok US outcome.
  - How fast Google AI Mode and Amazon agentic ads ramp.
