## 4. Most important difference

Both arms have identical prices and earnings dates. The gap is in the 52-week ranges and filings. Arm A had no quote API, so it took closing-basis 52-week ranges from equibles.com, and all 8 "% of range" figures fall outside tolerance (AAPL and MSFT show 100%, against 96.0% and 95.0%). Its filing lookups came from the same aggregator, which missed the later 8-Ks for GOOGL and NVDA. Arm B pulled ranges and filings from get_quote and get_filings and matched R0 on 6 of 8 ranges and every filing R0 covers. Its two misses (AAPL high 348.15, GOOGL low 244.15) look like Yahoo refreshing its 52-week fields after R0 was captured, not like errors. Strictly against R0, though, they are High-confidence misses and cost B a point on Fabrication. B also reported the true latest filing of any form type (Forms 4/144) and the latest periodic filing separately. That answers the question more literally than A did.
