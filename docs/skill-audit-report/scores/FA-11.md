## 4. Most important difference

Every financial figure is identical across the arms and matches R0, and both decks have native charts with 53 weekly points. The difference is how fragile arm A's data path was. With EDGAR, Yahoo, Stooq and Nasdaq blocked, A depended on one aggregator (stockanalysis.com) read through WebFetch's summarizing model. It had to stitch two adjusted-close snapshots with rescaling factors, and it marked the most recent quarters and the returns as Medium confidence because it could not cross-check them. Arm B pulled everything from four tool types in eight calls and finished in half the time (2.5 vs 5 minutes) at High confidence. The 1-year returns differ by 0.3–0.8pp only because of different window conventions, and neither can be scored against R0.
