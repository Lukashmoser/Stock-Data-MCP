## 4. Most important difference

Both arms have the same numbers. Arm A spent 48 WebFetch calls on stockanalysis.com, slickcharts, data.sec.gov and others to get there. Arm B needed 26 tool calls and 5 web calls. The real gap is in what each tool set can't reach. B's filing scan surfaced the two 10-13 insider filings (META Form 144, NVDA Form 4/A) that A's data.sec.gov fetch missed or couldn't type. A's web-heavy route, on the other hand, produced a far richer note: overnight headlines, price-target changes, CPI consensus, bank earnings and a sourced news driver for each name. B, with no newswire, wrote a price-and-filings-only note and left news, analyst actions and the bank calendar unverified. On the scored rubric the two arms tie. On usefulness as a morning note, A's content is stronger.
