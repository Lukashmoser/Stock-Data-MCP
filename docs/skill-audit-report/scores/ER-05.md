## 4. Most important difference

Both arms got every verifiable historical figure right, so accuracy doesn't separate them. The difference is in effort and depth. Arm A rebuilt three years of statements by fetching 23 individual SEC XBRL "R" pages, because direct EDGAR access was blocked. Arm B got the same history from 4 get_financials calls. It then used the saved effort to add a fourth historical year (FY2022) and twice as many quarters (8 vs 4), and it reconciled the FY2025 discontinued-operations cash flow that explains the ER-04 FCF gap (7,709 vs 6,493). The projections are close (FY2026E revenue within 0.6%), and both balance sheets balance in every period.
