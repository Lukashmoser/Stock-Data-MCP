## 4. Most important difference

Both arms got every verifiable figure right. The difference is in sourcing and precision. Arm A rebuilt the quarterly history from press releases and aggregators, rounded operating income to $100M, and took two of the four post-earnings moves from memory. Its capex figure came from an AI-generated call summary. Arm B took exact XBRL values for all four quarters from get_financials, computed every 1-day reaction from get_price_history close-to-close, and took capex from the cash-flow statement. As a result, every historical row in B is tool-sourced and High confidence. Consensus, guidance and AWS still came from the web in both arms, with different aggregators (Benzinga vs MarketBeat). That explains the spread on consensus revenue (202,090 vs 204,600) and on the base case.
