## 4. Most important difference

Both arms reached the same conclusion: SELL, with targets 7.5% apart (265 vs 285). Every verifiable market input is within tolerance in both. The real difference is how the inputs were derived. Arm B computed its own betas from 5 years of monthly prices, built peer LTM P/E from XBRL quarters, and took the risk-free rate straight from FRED, so every market input traces to a tool call. Arm A took published betas, P/Es and market caps from stockanalysis.com. That gave a higher beta (1.95 vs 1.59) and a WACC about 1.8 points higher, which explains most of the gap in DCF value. Both arms estimated peer NTM revenue themselves, so the comps leg is soft in both, and B marked it Low confidence.
