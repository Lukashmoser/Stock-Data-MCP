## 4. Most important difference

On data there is no difference. Both arms rolled forward to FY2026 with the 2026-10-15 close and produced identical numbers that all match R0 and R0c, and neither left a stale current-period value. Arm A got there from the 8-K earnings exhibit and a third-party price page; arm B got there with six stock-data calls. B also spotted the get_quote prev_close bug and used price history instead. The only real gap is mechanical. B rescaled the chart axis for the larger FY2026 bar, while A left it fixed at 320, so its new bar is visibly truncated even though A said the render was clean. This test does not show that the MCP made a material difference to accuracy. B was about a minute faster and needed no web scraping.
