## 4. Most important difference

The financial comparison is the same in both arms: every META and RDDT TTM ratio and the Alphabet ad total match to the decimal, because both arms pulled from the same filings. The MCP changed only the market data. Without it, arm A couldn't get a current META quote and used a cached Google Finance price from 2026-09-25. It also took RDDT from the 10-12 close, which put RDDT's market cap 3% above the reference. Arm B had exact 10-13 market caps from get_quote and filled the quarterly bases from get_financials instead of from memory. Neither arm could verify the segment ad lines or ByteDance, as expected. Those rows rest on press coverage and Sacra estimates in both arms, and the two arms' ByteDance valuations differ by USD 70bn.
