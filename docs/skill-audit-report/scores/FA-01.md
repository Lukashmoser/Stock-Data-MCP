## 4. Most important difference

There is no accuracy difference. Both arms reproduced every one of the 44 historical figures exactly and delivered fully balancing, cash-tied workbooks. The difference is in provenance and effort: arm A could not reach the 10-K on sec.gov and relied on Microsoft's earnings-release tables plus memory for opening balances and filing dates (which happened to be right), whereas arm B anchored on stock-data get_financials, then fetched XBRL R-pages to confirm and fill line items the MCP doesn't carry (D&A, lease and unearned-revenue detail), giving exact filing dates and accessions. The MCP improved auditability, not the numbers.
