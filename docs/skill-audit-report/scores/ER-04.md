## 4. Most important difference

The figures are effectively the same. Both arms land on identical values for every row, including the unverifiable ones, and both miss R0 on FCF only because they used AMD's own continuing-operations definition (5,519) rather than OCF − capex (6,735). The difference is in provenance and calibration. Arm B pulled the filings, statements and quote from stock-data and cited a tool or filing on every row. It also noticed the 6,735 vs 5,519 gap and lowered its confidence to M. Arm A had to piece the data together from AMD IR pages and aggregator sites (sec.gov was blocked). It took FY2023 revenue from memory and marked the off-reference FCF as High, which costs it a point on sourcing and a point on fabrication.
