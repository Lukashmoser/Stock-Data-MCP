Tool errors and stale-data flags

No call failed and no response carried "stale": true. These are the gaps and oddities to know about before scoring.

  • Gap
    Derived Q4 quarters have no EPS or share counts. MSFT, NVDA, AMZN and GOOGL Q4 values for revenue and income lines are annual minus Q1–Q3, but EPS and shares come back null. Tests that need Q4 EPS (ER-07, FA-11) must compute or source it.
  • Gap
    D&A not returned for MSFT, GOOGL, ORCL or AMD. EBITDA for comps (FA-03), the DCF (FA-04) and AMD valuation (ER-06) cannot be built from the MCP alone for these names.
  • Gap
    ORCL: cost of revenue, gross profit, pretax income, long-term debt and total liabilities not reported. AMZN: total liabilities and FY2025 share repurchases not reported. META: short-term debt and shares outstanding not reported. GOOGL: PP&E not reported. AMD: total liabilities not reported.
  • Check
    Large non-operating gains in 2026 quarters. GOOGL Q2 2026 pretax income is $138,753M against $40,770M operating income, and AMZN Q2 2026 net income is $62,647M against $27,461M operating income. These are probably gains on equity holdings. Check them against the 10-Qs; they would distort any P/E built on reported LTM earnings.
  • Check
    AMD 10-K lookup returned the 10-K/A by default. A skill that takes the first result will cite the amendment. The original 10-K has the same filing date (2026-02-04), so date scoring is unaffected.
  • Info
    GOOGL and GOOG market caps ($4,326,706M and $4,274,607M) both imply about 12,230M shares, which is all classes priced at that line's price. Either figure covers all share classes for ER-11; they differ only by the class price.
  • Info
    Macro dates. DGS10 and SOFR latest are 2026-10-09 because 10-12 was Columbus Day and 10-13 isn't published yet. T10Y2Y has a null on 10-12 for the same reason. CPI has no October 2025 print (null in FRED); latest month is August 2026.
  • Info
    Index and ETF quotes return no market cap, as expected.
  • Info
    Consistency checks passed. FCF equals OCF minus capex for every ticker checked. MSFT FY2026, NVDA FY2026 and AMZN FY2025 quarters sum exactly to the annual figures; GOOGL FY2025 is off by $1M.
